Privacy Policy

HealthMate AI respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal and health-related information you provide while using our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use HealthMate AI, we may collect information that is necessary to provide and improve the services available through the application. This may include:

  • Name and username
  • Email address
  • Account and login information
  • Health and medical information provided by you
  • Medicine details and medicine schedules
  • Appointment details
  • Emergency contact information
  • Uploaded medical reports and documents
  • Feedback submitted through the application
  • Questions and information provided through the HealthMate AI Assistant

2. Health and Medical Information

HealthMate AI may store health-related information that you voluntarily provide, such as medical history, symptoms, allergies, health conditions, medicines, medical reports, and other health information.

Because health information can be sensitive, users should only provide information that is necessary for using the application's features.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected by HealthMate AI may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Maintain your personal health records
  • Store and retrieve medical reports
  • Manage medicines and medicine schedules
  • Provide medicine reminders
  • Manage appointments
  • Store emergency contact information
  • Provide AI-assisted health information
  • Generate summaries of information you provide
  • Process feedback and improve the application
  • Maintain application security
  • Prevent unauthorized access and misuse

4. HealthMate AI Assistant

HealthMate AI includes an AI-powered health assistance feature that can process information provided in your questions to generate general health-related responses, summaries, and recommendations.

Important Medical Disclaimer:

AI-generated information is provided for general informational and educational purposes only. It is not a replacement for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Do not rely solely on AI-generated information for emergency situations, diagnosis, medication changes, or other important medical decisions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional when appropriate.

5. Medical Reports and Uploaded Files

Users may upload medical reports and other health-related documents. Uploaded files are associated with the user's account and are intended to be accessible only by the owning user or an authorized administrator, according to the application's access controls.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the right to upload any document or information submitted to the application.

6. Account and Password Security

Account information is protected using appropriate security measures. Passwords are stored using secure password hashing and are not stored as readable plain text.

Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential and should not share their password with other people.

7. Forgot Password and Account Recovery

If you use the forgot-password feature, information required to verify your account may be processed for account recovery and password-reset purposes.

Account recovery information is used to help protect your account and restore access when you have forgotten your password.

8. Data Storage

Information submitted through the application may be stored in our database and associated file storage. This may include account details, health records, medicine information, appointment information, reports, emergency contacts, feedback, and AI-related interactions.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

9. Access to Your Information

Private records are protected by account authorization. Users can access information associated with their own account through the application's authorized features.

Administrators may have limited access to information when required for legitimate system administration, security, maintenance, or support purposes.

10. Information Sharing

HealthMate AI does not intentionally sell your personal or health information.

Information may be processed or shared when reasonably necessary to provide requested functionality, operate required services, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

11. Third-Party Services and AI APIs

Some features of HealthMate AI may use third-party services or APIs. Where an external service is required to process information, only the information necessary for that feature should be transmitted.

API credentials and other confidential technical information are intended to be protected and should not be exposed through client-side code.

12. Cookies and Sessions

HealthMate AI may use cookies and session technologies to maintain authenticated sessions, support security features, and provide essential website functionality.

Disabling certain cookies or browser storage features may affect the functionality of some parts of the application.

13. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's services, maintain your account, support security, or satisfy applicable legal or administrative requirements.

When information is no longer required, appropriate measures may be taken to delete or securely dispose of it.

14. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the features available in the application, you may be able to review or update information associated with your account.

You may also contact the application administrator regarding questions about your information or requests concerning your account data.

15. User Responsibilities

To help protect your information, you should:

  • Keep your password confidential
  • Use a secure password
  • Log out when using a shared or public device
  • Provide accurate information
  • Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information
  • Do not attempt to access another user's account
  • Do not upload unauthorized or malicious files
  • Verify important AI-generated health information with a healthcare professional

16. Children's Privacy

HealthMate AI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization or consent where required by applicable law.

17. Data Security Limitations

We take reasonable steps to protect your information; however, no internet-based application or electronic storage system can guarantee complete security.

Users should understand that no method of transmitting or storing information online is completely risk-free.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, technology, security practices, or applicable requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

19. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact the HealthMate AI administrator through the contact information provided on the website.

20. Acceptance of This Privacy Policy

By creating an account or using HealthMate AI, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Where additional consent is required for specific types of information or processing, the application may request consent separately.
