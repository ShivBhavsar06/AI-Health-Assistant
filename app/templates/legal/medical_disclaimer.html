Medical Disclaimer

HealthMate AI is designed to provide general health information and health-management assistance. The information and features available through this application are intended to support organization and general awareness and are not intended to replace professional medical care.

1. Not a Substitute for Medical Advice

HealthMate AI does not replace a qualified doctor, physician, pharmacist, nurse, hospital, or other licensed healthcare professional.

Information provided by the application should not be considered professional medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice.

Users should consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional before making important decisions regarding their health or treatment.

2. HealthMate AI Assistant

The HealthMate AI Assistant may provide general health-related information, explanations, summaries, and recommendations based on the information provided by the user.

Important:

The AI Assistant is not a doctor and does not have the ability to perform a physical examination, independently verify all medical information, or provide a definitive medical diagnosis.

AI-generated responses may sometimes be incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, or inappropriate for a particular individual.

3. No Diagnosis

HealthMate AI does not provide definitive diagnoses of medical conditions.

Any information about symptoms, conditions, diseases, or possible health concerns should be treated as general educational information and not as confirmation that a user has a particular medical condition.

4. No Prescription or Treatment Decisions

HealthMate AI does not prescribe medicines or independently determine the appropriate treatment for a user.

Users should not start, stop, increase, decrease, or change medication based solely on information generated by the AI Assistant.

Medication-related decisions should be discussed with a qualified doctor, pharmacist, or other appropriate healthcare professional.

5. Medicine Information and Reminders

HealthMate AI may provide medicine-management features such as medicine records, dosage information entered by users, schedules, and reminders.

These features are intended to help users organize information and remember schedules. They do not guarantee that a medicine will be taken correctly or that a reminder will always be received, displayed, or noticed.

Users should verify medicine names, dosages, instructions, and schedules with the appropriate healthcare professional or official medication information.

6. Medical Reports and Health Records

HealthMate AI may allow users to store and organize medical reports and health records.

The application does not independently verify the accuracy, completeness, or authenticity of uploaded medical documents or information entered by users.

Users should rely on qualified healthcare professionals when interpreting medical reports or making healthcare decisions.

7. Emergency Situations

Do not use HealthMate AI for emergencies.

HealthMate AI is not an emergency medical service and cannot provide immediate emergency assistance.

If you believe that you or another person is experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services or seek immediate medical attention.

8. Individual Differences

Health information can vary significantly between individuals. Information that may be appropriate for one person may not be appropriate for another person because of factors such as medical history, existing conditions, allergies, medications, age, or other circumstances.

Users should therefore consider their individual circumstances and consult a healthcare professional when making medical decisions.

9. Accuracy of Information

Although reasonable efforts may be made to provide useful information, HealthMate AI does not guarantee that all information provided by the application is complete, accurate, current, or suitable for every user.

Users should independently verify important medical information using reliable medical sources or by consulting an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

10. User-Provided Information

AI responses and other application features may depend on information entered or uploaded by the user.

Incorrect, incomplete, outdated, or misleading information provided by a user may affect the usefulness or accuracy of the resulting information.

11. No Doctor-Patient Relationship

Use of HealthMate AI does not create a doctor-patient, pharmacist-patient, or other professional healthcare relationship between the user and HealthMate AI, its software, or its AI Assistant.

12. Professional Consultation

Users should seek professional medical advice when they have concerns about symptoms, diagnoses, medicines, treatment options, test results, or other health-related matters.

The AI Assistant may help users understand general health information, but professional healthcare providers should be consulted for personalized medical decisions.

13. Limitation of Reliance

Users should not rely solely on HealthMate AI when making decisions that could affect their health or safety.

The application should be used as a supporting information and organization tool rather than as a replacement for professional healthcare services.

14. Changes to This Disclaimer

This Medical Disclaimer may be updated when the features, functionality, AI capabilities, or intended use of HealthMate AI change.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised Last updated date.

15. Acceptance

By using HealthMate AI, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Medical Disclaimer and agree that the application is intended for general health information and health-management assistance rather than professional medical care.
